  • About Me!

    Just your classic illustrator who loves making fun characters to instill more joy in the day!

    Just me chilling right next to a promotional model of the Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom T-rex right outside the Fukui Prefectural Dinosaur Museum in Japan, probably my favorite museum ever Born 1999 and the last one out I arrived to the world in a crowd, he was the last of his triplet brothers. Since then I have always gone against the grain. From sticking to my childhood interests well into childhood, keeping his sense of wonder and positive vibes I believe sticking to my ideals is the key to living a fulfilling fun life. Since I could hold a pencil I have been drawing for the longest time. Even when I was told to focus on school I only half listened and drew on my school notes to the point I was told to cut it out by my teachers. This transgression paid off since once I graduated I went to Langara College for the Comix and Graphic Novel Program to learn how to make comics and then expanded my skills to include design at the Design Formation Program there. Now that I have maxed out my skills by graduating from Capilano University from the IDEA Program I am ready to take on any challenges that come my way be it finding work or doing the work.
